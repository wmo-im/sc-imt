== Ad hoc Task Team on WIGOS Metadata Representration 2

=== Terms of Reference

[loweralpha]
In close collaboration with SC-ON and coordination with the Secretariat:

. Develop, as a first step, a minimum viable product (MVP) next generation representation and encoding Standard for the WIGOS Metadata Representation (WMDR2) based on mandatory elements of WMO-No. 1192, consisting of:
.. Standard document
.. WMDR2 schema
.. Examples
.. Reference Implementation of working software (pywmdr)
. Ensure the Standard supports broad interoperability and is extensible for future development and iterations.
. Ensure the Standard is compatible with WIS2 architecture and metadata (WCMP2).
. Ensure that the MVP addresses the needs of the users as identified in the 2025 Ad hoc Task Team for OSCAR/Surface
. Draft the WMDR2 MVP for submission to INFCOM-4.
. Prepare and present/demonstrate at INFCOM-4.
